Research Themes

My research is organized around several interconnected themes, including mechanics-based simulation, soft robotics and adaptive structure fabrication, and robotic control.

Modeling

Mechanical Simulation

Mechanics-based and lumped-parameter modeling for soft robotic and reconfigurable systems, with an emphasis on fast computation and accurate validation.

Fabrication

Soft Robotics & Adaptive Structure Fabrication

Design and fabrication of soft robotic and adaptive structural systems using 3D printing, laser cutting, and modular fabrication methods.

Control

Arduino & Learning-Based Control

Arduino-based sensing and control integrating sensors, pumps, valves, motors, and robotic hardware, with learning-based control for robotic deployment of origami structures.
