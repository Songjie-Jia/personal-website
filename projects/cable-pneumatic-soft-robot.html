Cable-Pneumatic Soft Robot

An integrated fabrication and simulation framework for a newly developed cable-pneumatic soft robotic system.

Overview

A cable-pneumatic soft robotic system combining pneumatic actuation for large shape morphing with cable actuation for controllable motion. An integrated fabrication and simulation framework was developed for the system.

Background

Pneumatic actuation enables large deformation and compliant motion, while cable actuation provides more precise and responsive control. Existing hybrid systems remain limited in flexibility and efficient simulation of coupled pneumatic–cable mechanics.

Technical Approach

The robot combines a fabricated inflatable pneumatic core, cable actuation, and Arduino-based pressure and motor control. A lumped-parameter model captures membrane deformation, bending, pressure, and cable forces with substantially fewer degrees of freedom than conventional FEM.

Results

Experiments validated the model against bending stiffness and actuation-angle measurements, while demonstrating substantially faster computation than conventional FEM. Physical prototypes further demonstrated adaptive grasping, omnidirectional bending, manipulation, and UR5e cobot co-simulation.
