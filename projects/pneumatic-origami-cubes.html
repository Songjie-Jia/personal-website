Modular Self-Reconfigurable Structure

Modular reconfigurable structures using origami-inspired skeletons and inflatable actuators.

Overview

A modular, reusable, and self-deployable structural system combining origami-inspired skeletons with pneumatic actuators for adaptive structural configurations.

Background

Conventional deployable structures typically have a single prescribed configuration. The BLOC system introduces modularity and self-deployment to enable compact transport, reconfiguration, and reuse.

Technical Approach

Each BLOC unit combines two Sarrus linkages, thin-film pneumatic actuators, and modular connectors. A lumped-parameter model captures deployment and load-carrying behavior and is verified through experiments.

Results

The model predicts deployment and lifting capacity under varying pressure and load conditions. The same BLOC units were reconfigured into self-deploying beam, frame, and arch structures, with additional simulations of complex 3D assemblies.
